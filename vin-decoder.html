About this VIN decoder

Every car sold in the U.S. since 1981 carries a standardized 17-character VIN. This tool decodes it using the NHTSA vPIC (Vehicle Product Information Catalog) API — the same free, official database maintained by the U.S. National Highway Traffic Safety Administration — and then checks the NHTSA recalls database for safety campaigns affecting that year, make and model. No account, no fee, and your VIN is sent only to NHTSA's API.

Before buying a used car, compare the decoded details against the seller's listing: the year, engine, trim and body style should all match. A mismatch — or a VIN plate that differs between the windshield, door jamb and title — is a serious red flag. Pair this check with our used-car cost calculator and inspection checklist before you hand over any money. Note: vPIC coverage is strongest for U.S.-market vehicles.
