Why the "cheap" car is often the expensive one

Taxes, title and registration, dealer documentation fees and the first year of insurance, fuel and maintenance routinely add 25–40% on top of a used car's asking price. This calculator makes that visible before you commit, using your own local tax rate and fee estimates — adjust every field and the totals update instantly, entirely in your browser.

The checklist below it is the same walk-around a professional buyer does at auction: body and paint consistency, cold-start behaviour, fluid colours, warning lights, a structured test drive, and the paperwork checks that catch salvage titles and odometer problems. Tick items off on your phone as you inspect. Anything you find is either a walk-away reason or a documented reason to negotiate the price down.
