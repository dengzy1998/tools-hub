How the matching works

This checker extracts the most frequent meaningful words and two-word phrases from the job description (things like "project management", "data analysis" or "sql"), then checks which of them appear in your resume. The match rate is the share of those job keywords your resume already contains. It also scans your resume against a built-in library of 140+ common professional skills to show the strongest skill words you already have. Everything is computed locally in your browser — your resume is never uploaded anywhere.

A high score does not guarantee an interview, and you should never stuff in keywords for things you cannot actually do. Use the missing list honestly: if you genuinely have the experience, reword your bullets using the employer's own language; if you do not, that gap is a signal about fit, not a formatting problem. Pair this with the format checklist above — a keyword-perfect resume in a layout the parser cannot read still scores zero.
