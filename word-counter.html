About this word counter

Writers, students and marketers use word counts every day — for essays with strict limits, articles, product descriptions, social posts and speeches. This counter updates live as you type and also shows characters (with and without spaces), sentences, paragraphs and an estimated reading time at 200 words per minute.

The built-in text tools fix common annoyances in one click: convert a whole document between UPPERCASE, lowercase, Title Case and Sentence case; remove duplicate lines from lists and data exports; sort lines alphabetically; trim stray whitespace and collapse repeated spaces. Because it all happens in your browser, even sensitive drafts stay private.
