Etsy Listing Title & Tag Generator

Etsy gives you 140 characters for a title and exactly 13 tags of 55 characters each — and most sellers waste half of them. Fill in what you sell and get titles and tags that use the full allowance without ever going over.

How Etsy titles and tags actually work

Etsy search weighs the first words of your title and your 13 tags heavily, and tags work best as exact multi-word phrases a buyer would type — "soy candle gift for mom" beats three separate tags for "soy", "candle" and "gift". This generator builds titles from your product, material, style, audience and occasion, trims them at phrase boundaries so they never exceed 140 characters, and composes 13 tags that each stay inside the 55-character tag limit, favouring specific long-tail phrases over single generic words.

Use the character counters as a budget: green means the text fits, red means Etsy would cut it off. Generate a set, pick the title that reads most naturally to a human (search engines rank it, people click it), and copy the tags straight into your listing. Everything is generated locally in your browser from built-in category word banks — no account, no uploads.
