How this image compressor works

Drop in an image and this tool redraws it in your browser at the quality and size you choose, then encodes it as JPG, WebP or PNG. Because everything runs on your own device, there is no upload queue and no privacy risk — the original file is never sent anywhere.

For photographs, JPG at 75–85% quality usually cuts file size by 60–80% with no visible difference. WebP is often smaller still and is supported by all modern browsers. Use PNG when you need transparency or pixel-perfect graphics. Lower the max width/height to shrink huge phone photos before uploading them to websites, email or social media.
